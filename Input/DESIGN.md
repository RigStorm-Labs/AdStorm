01. BRAND POSITIONING
------------------------------------------------------------

Brand:
AdStorm

Category:
Digital Marketing Service Platform

Parent:
RigStorm Group of Companies

Core idea:
AdStorm takes a business, understands its goals, builds its
marketing presence, and strategically allocates its campaign
budget across the channels and activities that make sense.

AdStorm is NOT:
- An advertising marketplace
- An ad listing website
- An advertising ID platform
- An ad-revenue analytics platform
- A self-service ad-tech platform

AdStorm IS:
- A managed digital marketing service
- A business growth partner
- A marketing execution platform


02. BRAND PERSONALITY
------------------------------------------------------------

Bold
Modern
Strategic

Commercial

Generic SaaS

AI-generated startup aesthetic
Overly futuristic interfaces
Excessive gradients
Neon overload
Stock-photo agency aesthetic
Generic "digital marketing agency" templates


03. VISUAL RELATIONSHIP TO SITEmARKET
------------------------------------------------------------

AdStorm belongs to the same visual ecosystem as SiteMarket.

Shared DNA:
- Black
- Off-white
- Electric lime
- Bold geometric typography
- Strong negative space
- High contrast
- Minimal composition
- Confident oversized typography
- Clean geometric forms
- Sharp visual hierarchy

AdStorm must NOT copy:
- SiteMarket logo
- SiteMarket symbol
- Exact layouts
- Exact components
- Exact typography treatment

SiteMarket = digital creation.

AdStorm = digital growth.

The websites should look like sister companies,
not duplicate websites.

05. TYPOGRAPHY
------------------------------------------------------------

Typography philosophy:

Bold geometric display typography +
highly readable neutral body typography.

Display:
Heavy / Extra Bold geometric sans-serif

Recommended direction:
Satoshi / Geist / Inter-style geometric sans

H1:
72–96px desktop
44–58px mobile
Weight: 800–900
Line height: 0.92–1.02

H2:
48–64px desktop
36–44px mobile
Weight: 750–850

H3:
28–36px
Weight: 700–800

Body Large:
18–20px
Line height: 1.5

Body:
15–17px
Line height: 1.55

Small:
13–14px
Line height: 1.4

Label:
11–13px
Weight: 600–700
Letter spacing: 0.06em–0.12em

Metrics:
Use heavy geometric numerals.

Typography principles:

- Large headlines
- Short sentences
- Strong contrast
- Avoid excessive text density
- Never use five different fonts
- Never use decorative fonts

07. LAYOUT
------------------------------------------------------------

Use:

- Large editorial sections
- Asymmetric compositions
- Strong grid alignment
- Generous whitespace
- Large typography
- Occasional oversized visual elements

Avoid:

- Everything centered
- Endless card grids
- Tiny UI everywhere
- Excessive rounded containers
- Dashboard-style homepage


08. BORDER RADIUS
------------------------------------------------------------

Primary cards:
16px

Secondary cards:
12px

Buttons:
10–14px

Inputs:
10–14px

Large feature containers:
20–24px

Do not use excessive pill-shaped UI.

Pills should be reserved for:
- Tags
- Status
- Small metadata


09. BUTTON SYSTEM
------------------------------------------------------------

Primary:

Background:
Electric Lime

Text:
Black

Style:
Bold, compact, high contrast.

Example:
[ Start Your Growth Brief → ]

Secondary:

Background:
Transparent

Border:
Black/white depending on context

Example:
[ Explore Packages ]

Dark CTA:

Black background
Off-white text

Hover:

Primary:
Slight upward movement + subtle brightness change

Secondary:
Background transition

Motion:
150–250ms


10. CARDS
------------------------------------------------------------

Cards should communicate information,
not simply decorate the page.

Use cards for:

- Packages
- Services
- Process steps
- Deliverables
- Results/metrics
- FAQs
- Testimonials

Avoid:
Card inside card inside card.

Every card must have a reason to exist.


11. MOTION
------------------------------------------------------------

Motion should feel intentional.

Allowed:

- Fade + translate
- Slight scale
- Staggered entrance
- Number transitions
- Hover movement
- Navigation transitions
- Scroll reveal

Duration:
150–500ms

Easing:
ease-out / cubic-bezier

Avoid:

- Constant floating objects
- Excessive parallax
- Cursor gimmicks
- Particle backgrounds
- Huge GSAP-style animations
- Anything that makes the site feel like an AI demo


12. ICONOGRAPHY
------------------------------------------------------------

Use simple geometric icons.

Preferred:
Inline SVG.

Avoid:
Emoji as UI icons.

Avoid:
Generic 3D AI icons.

Icons should support the content,
not become the visual identity.


13. IMAGERY
------------------------------------------------------------

Photography should feel:

Real
Commercial
Human
Local-business oriented
Confident

Avoid:

Overused corporate stock photography
AI-generated people
Fake dashboards
Generic marketing illustrations

When no image is needed:
Use typography + geometric composition instead.


14. FORMS
------------------------------------------------------------

Form style:

Off-white background
Black text
Strong labels
Large comfortable inputs
Clear focus state
Lime CTA

Inputs:
Minimum 48–52px height.

Required fields:
Clearly marked.

Never make the customer wonder:
"What do you want me to enter here?"

Every field should have a business reason.


15. ACCESSIBILITY
------------------------------------------------------------

Required:

Semantic HTML
Labels for all form controls
Keyboard navigation
Visible focus states
Alt text for meaningful images
Decorative images marked appropriately
Readable contrast
Reduced-motion support
Mobile-friendly touch targets
No information conveyed by color alone


16. RESPONSIVE BREAKPOINTS
------------------------------------------------------------

Mobile:
<640px

640–1024px

Desktop:
>1024px

>1440px

not simply compressed from desktop.
